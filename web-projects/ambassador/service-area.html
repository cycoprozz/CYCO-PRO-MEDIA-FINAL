Service Areas

Ambassador Limousine serves all of metro Atlanta and the surrounding region with premium chauffeured transportation.

Where We Serve

From Hartsfield-Jackson Airport to Alpharetta, from Augusta to Peachtree City — Ambassador Limousine covers the entire metro Atlanta area and beyond. If you're in Georgia, we can get you there in style.

Hartsfield-Jackson Airport

The world's busiest airport — we specialize in ATL transfers with flight tracking and meet-and-greet.

Alpharetta

North Fulton's technology hub. Executive transportation for corporations and residents throughout Alpharetta.

Augusta

Atlanta to Augusta transportation — especially popular during The Masters Tournament each April.

Duluth

Gwinnett County transportation serving Duluth's diverse community and Gas South Arena.

Lawrenceville

Gwinnett County seat — airport transfers, corporate service, and event transportation.

Marietta

Cobb County's hub — serving Marietta Square, Cumberland corridor, and Truist Park.

Mercedes-Benz Stadium

Falcons, Atlanta United, and FIFA World Cup 2026 venue transportation service.

Peachtree City

South Fulton's premier planned community — airport transfers and executive transportation.

Roswell

Historic Canton Street charm — full-service luxury transportation throughout Roswell and North Fulton.

Sandy Springs

Perimeter Center corridor — preferred car service for major corporate headquarters in Sandy Springs.

Smyrna

Cobb County's Market Village — luxury transportation for Smyrna residents and Truist Park visitors.

Truist Park

Atlanta Braves and The Battery Atlanta — game day and event transportation to Truist Park.

Don't See Your City?

Ambassador Limousine serves all of metro Atlanta and beyond. If your location isn't listed above, contact us — we almost certainly serve your area.
