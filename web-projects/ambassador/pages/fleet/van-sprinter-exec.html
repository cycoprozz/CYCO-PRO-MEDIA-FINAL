About the Mercedes Sprinter Executive Van

The Mercedes Sprinter Executive Van is purpose-built for business groups who need a mobile office on wheels. With seating for up to 12 executives in genuine comfort, onboard Wi-Fi, fold-out work tables, and power at every seat, your team can remain productive from airport pickup to final destination.

This vehicle is a favorite for corporate retreats, roadshows, conference groups, and executive shuttle services. The generous luggage capacity makes it equally popular for airport group transfers with substantial bags.
