You are responsible for providing accurate pickup time, pickup location, drop-off location, and number of passengers. Ambassador Limousine is not responsible for delays or additional charges resulting from inaccurate information provided at the time of booking.

3. Payment

Payment is required at the time of booking for standard reservations. We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) processed through our secure payment system. Corporate accounts may arrange invoicing on NET 30 terms with prior approval.

All quoted rates are in US dollars and include base charges. Gratuity (18–20%), applicable tolls, and parking fees will be itemized and added to the final invoice.

4. Cancellation Policy

Standard Reservations

  • Cancellations 24+ hours before scheduled pickup: Full refund
  • Cancellations within 24 hours: 50% cancellation fee
  • No-shows: Full trip charge applies

Special Event Reservations (Weddings, Proms, Large Group)

  • Cancellations 7+ days before event: Full refund less deposit
  • Cancellations 3–6 days before: 50% of total charge
  • Cancellations within 72 hours: Full charge

5. Wait Time and Additional Charges

Airport pickups include a complimentary wait time of 45 minutes from the actual flight arrival time. Standard pickups include 15 minutes of complimentary wait time. Wait time beyond these limits is billed at the prevailing hourly rate for the vehicle.

Extra stops, route changes requested during the trip, and tolls incurred during the ride will be added to the final invoice.
