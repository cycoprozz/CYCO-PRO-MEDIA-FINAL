The Atlanta Airport Transfer You Can Count On

Getting to and from Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport (ATL) — the world's busiest airport — can be a stressful experience. Traffic on I-85, I-285, and I-75 is unpredictable at the best of times. Parking is expensive, remote, and time-consuming. Rideshare apps leave you waiting in curbside chaos.

Ambassador Limousine eliminates all of that. Our professional chauffeurs monitor your flight in real-time and arrive ready — whether your flight is on time, early, or delayed. You'll be greeted at baggage claim with a personalized sign and escorted to your vehicle with your luggage handled.

What's Included

  • Real-time flight tracking — we adjust automatically for delays
  • 45-minute complimentary wait time from actual arrival
  • Meet-and-greet at baggage claim with name sign
  • Luggage assistance and vehicle loading
  • All terminals served: T, A, B, C, D, E, F and the Maynard H. Jackson International Terminal
  • Complimentary bottled water in every vehicle
  • Professional, uniformed chauffeur
  • Late-model luxury vehicles — sedans, SUVs, Sprinter vans, and buses
  • No surge pricing — your quote is locked in at booking
  • 24/7 availability, 365 days including holidays
