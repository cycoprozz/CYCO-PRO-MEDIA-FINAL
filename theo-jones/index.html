The Ritual

More Than Hair.
A Whole Wellness Session.

At Theo Jones, every appointment is designed as an elevated self-care experience. We begin with intention, consultation, and education — then move into customized hair care, relaxation, styling, and a plan that helps you maintain your results beyond the chair.

Step 01

Personal Consultation

We listen to your lifestyle, hair goals, concerns, and maintenance needs. No assumptions — only intention.

Step 02

Hair & Scalp Education

We explain what your hair needs, why it needs it, and how to care for it at home so you leave truly informed.

Step 03

Spa-Inspired Shampoo Ritual

A calming cleanse, scalp attention, and restorative moment before styling begins.

Step 04

Elevated Styling & Finish

A polished, confidence-building finish designed around your personal beauty and unique expression.

Step 05

Aftercare Coaching

You leave with guidance, product recommendations, and a personalized plan for healthy, thriving hair.
