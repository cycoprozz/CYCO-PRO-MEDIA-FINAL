Wedding Transportation

Arrive in elegance on your most special day. Luxury wedding car service throughout Atlanta and beyond.

Make Your Wedding Day Unforgettable

Your wedding day deserves nothing less than perfection. Ambassador Limousine has been Atlanta's trusted wedding transportation provider since 2012, ensuring brides, grooms, and their entire wedding parties arrive in style, comfort, and on time.

From the intimate elopement to the grand multi-venue celebration, we coordinate every transportation detail so you can focus on what matters most. Our white-glove wedding chauffeurs understand the emotional significance of your day and are trained to deliver exceptional, stress-free service.

Wedding Package Includes

  • Dedicated wedding transportation coordinator
  • Formal-attired chauffeur trained in wedding protocols
  • Red carpet service and door-to-door assistance
  • Chilled champagne or sparkling cider for the couple
  • Decorative floral arrangement on vehicle (available)
  • Complimentary "Just Married" decoration option
  • Multi-stop service (ceremony, photos, reception)
  • Bridal party fleet coordination for large weddings
  • Backup vehicle on standby for your peace of mind
  • Flexible hourly packages to cover your entire day
