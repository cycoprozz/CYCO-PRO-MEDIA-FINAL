Our Story

About Ambassador Limousine

Over a decade of setting the standard for premium transportation in Atlanta, Georgia — and beyond.

Who We Are

Atlanta's Most Trusted Name in Luxury Transportation

Founded in 2012 with a simple but ambitious mission — to redefine what a limousine service could be in Atlanta — Ambassador Limousine has grown into the region's most respected premium transportation company.

We built our reputation one ride at a time, earning the loyalty of C-suite executives, wedding planners, touring artists, sports teams, government officials, and everyday people celebrating life's most meaningful moments.

Our headquarters at 3355 Lenox Rd NE in Atlanta's prestigious Buckhead neighborhood puts us at the heart of the city we serve, with teams available to dispatch 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Our Chauffeurs

People Who Take Pride in Every Mile

Our chauffeurs aren't just drivers — they're professionals who take genuine pride in their work. Each one completes a rigorous onboarding process including background checks, defensive driving courses, and an in-person ride-along with a senior team member before their first independent assignment.

They're trained in professional conduct, airport protocols, luggage handling, and discretion. Whether you're a Fortune 500 CEO or celebrating your wedding day, you'll receive the same attentive, white-glove service.
