Corporate Transportation

Executive ground transportation for Atlanta's business community — punctual, professional, and discreet.

Atlanta's Premier Corporate Car Service

Ambassador Limousine understands that in the business world, punctuality and professionalism are non-negotiable. Our corporate transportation service is built around the needs of Atlanta's executives, corporations, and business travelers who demand reliability and discretion every time.

Whether you need airport transfers for visiting clients, daily executive commutes, roadshow logistics, or multi-vehicle corporate event transportation, our professional chauffeurs and late-model fleet deliver a consistent, five-star experience.

Corporate Service Includes

  • Professional, background-checked chauffeurs in formal attire
  • Real-time flight tracking for all airport pickups
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi in select vehicles for productivity on the go
  • Chilled bottled water and amenities on board
  • Meet-and-greet service with name placards
  • Corporate account management with monthly invoicing
  • Centralized billing and detailed trip reporting
  • Dedicated account manager for multi-vehicle bookings
  • 24/7 dispatch for last-minute schedule changes
  • Confidential, discreet service for C-suite and VIP clients
